One India. Many voices.

The vision

One devotional evening, carried across eight cities of India.

Each city adds its own music, its own young voices and its own prasadam. Every ticket also pays for a child's education.

One country.
Many languages.
Many musical traditions.
One devotion.
Four pillars
Bhakti and raga

Devotion everyone can sing, with the depth of raga.

Bhajans and kirtans of Krishna, Rama and Devi. Raga gives each one its mood. It stays a bhajan evening, never a formal recital.

The journey

Eight cities. Eight sounds.

One evening

Two hours and fifty minutes, from welcome to kirtan.

The signature moment

One medley that walks across India.

Hindi is the thread. Each city adds its own language.

A day at the Utsav

The music starts long before the concert.

Udaan

Young voices sing first.

Student choirs, folk dance, tabla, harmonium, flute and poetry from local schools and academies. Students with visual impairment join by choice, with full support. They come as artists, never as a cause.

Handmade with love

A market where every stall tells you who made it.

Diyas, candles, cards, pottery, textiles and art by students and local trusts. Student stalls pay no fee. Each stall says where its money goes.

Prasadam

Every city feeds you its own prasadam.
